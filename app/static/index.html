The farmer is alerted when the chance of frost on the plot is 30% or more.

Rule: Alert if P(frost) ≥ 30% (text and voice note; at most 2 alerts per plot every 7 days) · Watch if the plot has no station nearby, the cold end of the range is ≤ 0 °C and P is under 30% (text only, no voice note, does not count toward the cap) · sent 18:00–20:00. Basis: «Station-anchored» = plot within 1.5 km of a weather-service (SMN) station (validated setting); «Terrain transfer» = no station nearby, wide range (dashed outline on the map); «Thermometer (k nights)» = calibrated with the plot's own readings. Names and phone numbers are fictional; coordinates are real.
