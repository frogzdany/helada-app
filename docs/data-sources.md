# Data sources

Every dataset Helada was built or tested with, and **what each one does not cover**.

## Datasets used to build Helada

Sizes are what is in this repository, unless a row says the cache is gitignored. Gitignored caches are rebuilt with `model/research/01-04_*.py`. [V] = not verified.

| Dataset | Source | License | Size | Used for | What it does **not** cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SMN/CONAGUA daily station climatology** (observed Tmin and Tmax) | [smn.conagua.gob.mx](https://smn.conagua.gob.mx/es/climatologia/informacion-climatologica/informacion-estadistica-climatologica), one `dia{ID}.txt` file per station | Public government data, cite CONAGUA/SMN. The download page states no formal license [V] | 186 stations in the model domain (lat 18.9–20.1, lon −100.3 to −99.2, ≥ 2,300 m), records 1946–Sep 2026. In git: station tables (20 KB + 25 KB) and recent nights for the 16 demo stations (13 KB). The full daily cache is gitignored | Training target (night Tmin), backtest truth, frost-date climatology (103 stations with ≥ 10 good years), observed Tmin printed in the loss packet | **Few stations near parcels.** A parcel counts as "near" only within 1.5 km and 50 m of elevation; held-out stations sit a median 6 km from the next station. **Few stations above 2,800 m.** **Shelter height, not crop:** a manual 08:00 reading at about 1.5 m, while a maize canopy on a clear night is typically 1–2 °C colder. **Imprecise metadata:** for 15 of 184 stations the altitude disagrees with the DEM by more than 100 m. **Lag:** publication trails by about a week at most stations and by 3–10 months at some. **No quality flags:** we screen ranges, Tmin > Tmax, outliers and stuck values ourselves. **Frost clusters at a few valley-floor stations.** |
| **Open-Meteo Previous Runs, `ecmwf_ifs025` day-1 forecast** | [previous-runs-api.open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api), queried at each station's coordinates | CC BY 4.0. The free API tier is for non-commercial use | 83 stations, Mar 2024 – Sep 2026; 51,258 station-nights in the training table (gitignored cache) | Model input: the forecast the model corrects (Tmin, dew point, cloud, wind, afternoon Tmax) | **Only two frost seasons** (2024-25, 2025-26): the archive starts in 2024. **0.25° grid (about 25 km):** it cannot see a hollow; that is the gap the model fills. 8 of 91 stations are missing (the hourly rate limit hit them). **Advective frost is out of scope:** the model adds local offsets and night weather, so a cold air mass the forecast misses entirely is not corrected. We did not measure skill separately on advective nights. A real deployment needs a paid key or a self-hosted copy. |
| **Open-Meteo `best_match`** (the default blend) | Same API, default model | CC BY 4.0 | 91 stations, Feb 2024 – Sep 2026 (gitignored cache) | Baseline only: "what a free weather app shows" (24% of frosts caught) | Not a fixed model: the blend and its bias change between seasons (warm bias +3.4 °C in 2021-22, +1.5 °C in 2025-26). Not used as a model input. |
| **ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalysis** | [Open-Meteo archive API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api) (Copernicus C3S) | CC BY 4.0, attribution to Copernicus | Puno only: `model/regions/out/puno_nights.csv.gz` (154 KB, 10 points, Mar 2024 – Sep 2026) and `puno_eval.json` (36 KB) | Puno country swap: puts a floor under the uncertainty band | **It is not an observation.** It is a 9–31 km model reconstruction, and the two reanalyses disagree with each other almost as much as with the forecast (1.74 vs 1.83 °C). The coldest ERA5 night at our 10 points was −6.7 °C, while SENAMHI forecast −8 to −14 °C for the highlands in July 2025. **No skill claim in Puno.** Not used in Toluca. |
| **Copernicus DEM GLO-30** | [AWS Open Data](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/), 6 tiles N18–N20, W100–W101 | Copernicus DEM license: free, with attribution "© DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the EU and ESA" | `dem_6s.npz`, 865 KB (int16, block-averaged to 6″ ≈ 180 m), shipped with the model | 12 terrain features per parcel, offline; parcel elevation | **A surface model:** it includes buildings and trees. **About 180 m cells:** a 30 m version was tested and did not help. **Toluca domain only.** **Terrain explains little:** it recovered about 10% of the station-offset gap (|Spearman ρ| ≤ 0.3). INEGI's 15 m CEM was not used (manual download only). |
| **Test voice notes** | **Synthetic, made by the team**: macOS text-to-speech voices (Paulina, Eddy, Reed, Grandpa, Rocko, Flo; es-MX), encoded as WhatsApp-style OGG/Opus, 3 with noise added (`app/scripts/make_test_clips.py`) | Ours. The voices are Apple system voices; we use the clips for testing only [V: Apple's terms on redistributing TTS output] | 12 clips, 100 KB, plus `manifest.json` | Speech-recognition and slot-filling benchmark (`docs/small-ai-bench.md`) | **No real farmer speaks in them.** No rural accent, no wind on a cheap mic, no Mazahua code-switching. The vocabulary prompt was written while looking at these clips. The 2.7% WER is a lower bound, not field accuracy. |
| **Message probe sets** | Written by the team (`evals/probes/`), plus the 15 phrases in `app/tests/test_slots.py` | Ours (MIT, with the repo) | 10 files, 60 KB | Acceptance tests for the intent router and the slot rules | **Not real farmer messages.** The rules were written against some of these phrases, so scores on them are optimistic. A post-hoc held-out set exists (`local_holdout_det.json`, 26 messages), but it was also written by the team. Its first score, before any fix, was 73 of 80 checks (91%); the misses were then fixed, so it is no longer held out. |
| **Demo roster** | Ours (`app/backend/data/roster.json`) | Ours | 12 parcels, 6 KB | Demo and replay | **Names, phones and areas are fictional.** The coordinates are real points near SMN stations, but land use was not checked. Not a farmer registry. |
| **Pre-trained speech model**: faster-whisper `small`, int8 | OpenAI Whisper weights, converted by SYSTRAN/CTranslate2 | MIT | 486 MB | Voice note → text, on the box | Trained on web audio, not on rural Mexican Spanish. **No Mazahua.** Not fine-tuned by us. |
| **Pre-trained LLM, optional**: `qwen3:1.7b` Q4_K_M (also tested: `qwen3:0.6b`, `gemma3:1b`) | Alibaba Qwen; Google Gemma (through Ollama) | Qwen3: Apache 2.0. Gemma 3: Gemma Terms of Use | 1.4 GB (qwen3 1.7b) | Only as a recall helper behind the rules, for crop and cause | Off by default. Alone, it got 44% of slots confidently wrong (`docs/small-ai-bench.md`). |
| **Program rules: PASACME lineamientos** | [Gaceta del Gobierno, 25 Jun 2024](https://secampo.edomex.gob.mx/sites/secampo.edomex.gob.mx/files/files/Acerca%20de%20la%20Secretaria/Reglas%20de%20Operacion/Lineamientos_PASACME_2024.pdf) | Public official document | 17 pages (12 of rules, 5 of annexes); encoded in `app/backend/pasacme.yaml` | Packet checklist: 10-day clock, 60%, documents | **The 2026 changes** (MX$5,000 / 4 ha) are reported by the press but not published [V]. **The UGA restriction layer** was not obtained, so the packet says "verificar con la Delegación". |

### What no dataset here covers

- **Real farmer speech**, in Spanish or Mazahua.
- **Parcel-level observations** away from SMN stations. Loggers are the stated precondition (`model/README.md`, "Logger learning curve").
- **Crop damage on the ground.** We have no field-verified loss records. The PASACME inspection is the ground truth, and we do not see it.
- **More than two frost seasons** of forecast-versus-observation pairs.

### Short version

| Data | What it gives us | What it doesn't cover |
|---|---|---|
| 186 SMN weather stations | The real night minimum, 1946–2026 | Parcels far from a station |
| Archived forecasts (Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0) | What the forecast said the day before | Only 2 frost seasons; cold fronts it misses |
| Copernicus 30 m elevation map | Terrain for any parcel, offline | It explained little of the error |
| 12 test voice notes | A speech benchmark | **Synthetic voices**, not farmers |
| Puno reanalysis (ERA5) | An honest band in Peru | Not observations: no skill claim |
