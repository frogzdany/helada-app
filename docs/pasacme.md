# PASACME: verified rules and packet checklist

Status: verified 2026-09-29 against the primary source (Gaceta del Gobierno, 25 Jun 2024). Items marked [V] are unverified.

Primary source (read in full): Acuerdo de la Secretaría del Campo por el que se emiten los Lineamientos y Mecánica Operativa del Programa Atención de Siniestros Agroclimáticos en el Campo Mexiquense (PASACME), Periódico Oficial Gaceta del Gobierno, Tomo CCXVII No. 114, 25 junio 2024, signed 13 June 2024 (pp. 29-40).
https://secampo.edomex.gob.mx/sites/secampo.edomex.gob.mx/files/files/Acerca%20de%20la%20Secretaria/Reglas%20de%20Operacion/Lineamientos_PASACME_2024.pdf
Index of SECAMPO rules: https://secampo.edomex.gob.mx/reglas-operacion (lists only the 2024 PASACME document; 2025/2026 pages list other programs).

Note on naming: the program is "Lineamientos y Mecánica Operativa", not "Reglas de Operación". Name in full: Programa Atención de Siniestros Agroclimáticos en el Campo Mexiquense. Funded from FOMIMEX (state trust fund), not federal money.

## 1. What the 2024 lineamientos say (in force unless replaced)

| Item | Rule | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Personas físicas, "pequeños productores" of up to 3 ha (family labor, limited resources, mostly self-consumption). No personas jurídico-colectivas. | 4, 6.3, 9.2 |
| Crops | Anuales de temporal, hortalizas, frutales en producción, or others SECAMPO decides to include | 5.2.1, 6.3 |
| Amount | Up to MX$4,000 per hectare siniestrada (or proportional share by damage), up to 3 ha per beneficiary; subject to budget sufficiency | 8.3 |
| Eligible causes | "Siniestro agroclimático": sequía, helada, granizada, nevada, inundación, tornado (glossary). Objectives text also lists lluvias and viento | 4, Considerando |
| Minimum damage | Affectation of 60% or more of the cultivated (or harvest) surface | 9.3.1 e, 9.3.3 |
| Notice deadline | Farmer must inform the Delegación Regional de Desarrollo Agropecuario of the loss within the first 10 calendar days | 9.3.1 c |
| Pre-registration | Mandatory pre-registration of crop and surface in the program form (Google Form linked in the 2024 text: https://forms.gle/MWiobmsFZYdyXeGf8 ; whether it is still live was not checked [V]) and completion at the Delegación (Anexo 4) | 6.3, 10.3 |
| Restrictions | One claim per plot per fiscal year; not if plot is in restricted UGA zone (Ordenamiento Ecológico 2023); not if farmer owes SECAMPO; not if not a small producer | 9.2 |
| Priority order | Women heads of household; indigenous-community residents; people in poverty; those at risk/affected; high-productivity zones; older adults; SECAMPO strategic projects | 9.3.2 |
| Window | Ventanillas open from publication, business days 09:00-18:00, at Delegaciones Regionales. No fixed annual close in the text; the 10-day notice rule is the operative clock | 10.2 |
| Cost | Free. Filing does not guarantee support | 9.1, 11.1.4 |
| Payment | Direct electronic dispersal by payment order (DIE); beneficiary must attend on the date set or lose it after 10 business days | 10.4, 11.1.5 |
| Talks | Applicants receive talks on agroecology and insurance funds | 10.4 |

### How losses are verified today (this is the gap Helada fills)
The Instancia Ejecutora (Delegación) "verifica la afectación mediante la inspección en el lugar", attaches photographic evidence and fills the **Acta de verificación de siniestro (Anexo 3)** (9.3.3 e, 10.4). The dictamen must state: folio, type of siniestro, percent affectation (>= 60%), phenological stage of the crop, crop type, procedente or not with reasons, and amount (9.3.3). There is no weather-station or satellite requirement in the text. Helada's packet therefore never replaces the inspection; it pre-fills what the inspector needs (date, coordinates, photo, phenological stage, nearby-station Tmin) and shows how many of the 10 notice days are left.

### Delegaciones relevant to the Toluca valley (10.1)
- I Atlacomulco: Atlacomulco, Ixtlahuaca, Jiquipilco, Jocotitlán, San Felipe del Progreso, San José del Rincón, Temascalcingo, others.
- III Metepec: Almoloya de Juárez, Zinacantepec, Toluca, Metepec, Calimaya, Tenango del Valle, etc.; extension for Lerma, Ocoyoacac, Otzolotepec, Temoaya, Xonacatlán, etc.
- IV Valle de Bravo: Villa Victoria, Villa de Allende, others. VI Tenancingo (+ extension): Villa Guerrero.

What the app does: the cap is a config value, not hard-coded (`max_ha: 3` in `app/backend/pasacme.yaml`), and the checklist compares the declared area with it. The amount (`amount_per_ha: 4000`) is in the same file for reference only: no code reads it, and the packet never states an amount as an entitlement. Each packet ends with the note "Lineamientos PASACME 2024 (vigentes, verificados). 2026: 4 ha / $5,000 por ha anunciado en prensa, no publicado [V]."

## 3. Checklist the packet shows

Output rule: the status line reads "Documentos completos" or "Falta: X". If the farmer's answer about pre-registration is unclear, it adds "Confirmar: X". It never says "elegible": the code raises an error if the checklist text has eligibility wording. Under the status and on every page: "Lo decide la Secretaría del Campo."

A. Facts the packet fills from the roster, the voice note, the photos and the model. Each row names its origin: "padrón" (roster), "declarado" (said by the farmer), "Helada" (computed) or "pendiente" (left for the Delegación):
1. Productor (persona física), nombre, from the roster. Names in the demo are fictional, and the packet says so in a banner.
2. Municipio and the Delegación Regional that applies. The lookup in the config covers five municipalities from the list in §1; for any other the packet says "consultar".
3. Parcel coordinates (from the roster; GPS from photo EXIF or a shared location is compared with them) and surface declared, in ha, compared with the 3 ha cap (`max_ha`). A larger area is marked "Revisar".
4. Crop and phenological stage at the time of loss (required in the dictamen). The stage is estimated from the crop calendar; for maize, a stage the farmer names is shown too.
5. Cause (helada, granizada, sequía, inundación, nevada, tornado) and date of the event.
6. Days elapsed since the event and date by which the Delegación must be notified (event date + 10 calendar days). The packet shows "faltan N días".
7. Percentage of the surface affected, from the declared area and the parcel's area in the roster (threshold 60%: the packet shows "Declarado X%; el umbral de los lineamientos es 60%; lo verifica la inspección").
8. Photos: at least four photos of the damaged crop (9.1 c). The packet shows the count and a date and GPS check for each photo (EXIF, or the time of receipt and a shared location when WhatsApp has removed the EXIF).
9. Evidence of the event: modeled Tmin at the parcel, nearest SMN station observed Tmin (frost); hail/other causes: "Sin evidencia meteorológica automática para esta causa".

B. Documents the farmer must bring to the ventanilla (originals plus copies) (9.1). The packet prints them as empty checkboxes; Helada never receives them. The chat asks about one of them, the pre-registration (yes or no):
- Pre-registro en el formulario PASACME (a).
- FUR, Formato Único de Registro (Anexo 2), filled at the ventanilla (b).
- Identificación oficial vigente (INE, pasaporte, cartilla, cédula) (d).
- CURP actualizada (e).
- Comprobante de domicilio, not older than 3 months (f).
- Constancia de productor agrícola from comisariado ejidal, delegado or ayuntamiento, issued within the last 6 months (g).
- Proof of ownership or possession of the plot: título de propiedad; certificado parcelario/comunal or tribunal resolution; constancia de posesión from comisariado or presidente municipal; lease/comodato with two witnesses; albacea appointment; escritura pública (h). Non-notarized documents must carry the legend "Se expide únicamente para los fines de los Lineamientos y Mecánica Operativa del programa Atención de Siniestros Agroclimáticos en el Campo Mexiquense...".
- Rule 9.1: "No se tendrá por formulada la solicitud si no se presentan todos y cada uno de los documentos". The packet prints this line in bold.

C. Restrictions (9.2). Helada cannot verify them; the packet shows a row for each:
- Only one claim for this plot this year: the packet counts earlier Helada packets for the plot in the year; the Delegación verifies it.
- Plot is not in a restricted UGA: the UGA layer is in the Ordenamiento Ecológico 2023 and was not obtained, so the packet says "verificar con la Delegación".
- No outstanding debt with SECAMPO: "Se confirma en ventanilla".
- Person, not a group or company: taken from the roster (item A1).

D. Process box on the packet (what happens next): inspección en sitio by the Delegación with Acta de verificación (Anexo 3) -> dictamen -> notification -> payment by electronic order -> collect on the assigned date (10 business days or it counts as desistimiento).

E. Footer: "Este documento organiza información; no es una solicitud ni una resolución." "La Secretaría del Campo verifica en campo; este paquete no sustituye la inspección." "Lo decide la Secretaría del Campo." The packet uses the program name, so it also prints the program's mandatory legend: "Este programa es público, ajeno a cualquier partido político. Queda prohibido el uso para fines distintos a los establecidos en el programa" (Sec. 14). The legend is written for the program's own materials [V]. The version note (§2) comes last.

## 4. Limits
- Verification is by physical inspection; Helada speeds up pre-filling and timestamps, it does not certify a loss.
- The 60% threshold and the 10-day clock are the two facts a farmer is most likely to miss. The packet shows both.
- Priority criteria favor women heads of household and indigenous-community residents (9.3.2), so the design does not depend on the farmer owning a phone (see `docs/tradeoffs-preconditions.md`).
- Gaming risk: EXIF/GPS/station data raise the cost of fraud but do not eliminate it; the inspection remains.
- The 2026 rule change is unpublished [V]. The checklist uses the 2024 cap of 3 ha; MX$5,000 and 4 ha appear only in the packet's version note, as announced and not published.
